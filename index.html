About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Research Institute of Mathematics of Seoul National University and a member of the Dynamics and Arithmetic Structures on Manifolds (DASOM) research group.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow at Chennai Mathematical Institute. I completed my PhD in July 2026 at the Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai, under the supervision of Dr. Anup Dixit.

My research is in number theory, with particular interests in:
